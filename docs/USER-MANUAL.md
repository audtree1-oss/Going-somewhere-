# Going Somewhere! — The Owner's Manual 🚗💨

*Welcome, Keely. This is your app. Here's everything it does, how to use
it, and what's under the hood — in plain English, no tech degree required.*

---

## The big idea

You know the problem: **you** plan the trip, and then you spend the whole
trip being a human information desk. *"What are we doing today?" "What
time do we leave?" "Where's the hotel?"*

Going Somewhere! fixes that. Everyone on the trip opens the app and sees
today's plan — where to be, when to be ready, how long the drive is,
where you're sleeping tonight. Nobody has to ask. **You're off duty.**

Every travel app plans the trip. Yours *runs* it.

---

## Getting started

1. Open your app's web address on your phone.
2. Tap **Create an account** — name, email, password. Done.
3. **Pro tip:** on iPhone, open the site in Safari, tap the Share button,
   and choose **"Add to Home Screen."** Now it sits on your phone like a
   real app icon. (Android: Chrome menu → "Add to Home screen.")

Everyone who travels with you does the same — creating an account is free
and takes 30 seconds. They join *your* trip with an invite code (more on
that below).

## Making a trip

Tap the big orange **+** button.

- **Name it** — "Nashville or Bust," "Mom's Birthday Extravaganza"
- **Dates** — start and end. This is what builds your Day 1, Day 2, Day 3…
- **Cover emoji** — the trip's little badge (🎸 for a concert run,
  🏔️ for the mountains…)
- **Trip vibe** — Adventure, Concerts & Live Music, Shows & Theater,
  National Parks, Beach Escape… flavor for the trip
- Hit **LET'S GOOO!** 🎉 (yes, there's confetti)

After creating it, tap **Edit** (top right inside the trip) to add a
cover photo.

## The six tabs

Every trip has six tabs along the bottom:

### 🌞 Today — the heart of the whole app

Open the app on a trip day and this screen tells you everything:

- **The date and which day of the trip it is** ("Day 2 of 5")
- **Weather** for where you'll be
- **"Be ready by"** — worked out from your first stop's arrival time
  and the drive to get there
- **Total driving time and miles** for the day
- **The day as a timeline** — every stop in order, with drive times
  between them and one-tap **directions** links that open GPS navigation
- **Tonight's hotel** — address, check-in time, confirmation number,
  Wi-Fi password, parking notes
- **"If we have time"** — your wishlist ideas, waiting for a free hour
- **🎒 Pack for today** — a short list built automatically from the
  day's stops and weather: hiking day → hiking shoes and water; concert
  night → tickets and a charged phone; rain coming → umbrella; new
  hotel tonight → "pack up the room!"
- **⭑ UP NEXT** — on trip days, the next stop on the timeline is
  highlighted so one glance answers "what's next?"
- **"Who's ready?"** — a ✅ 3/6 counter right in the header
- **"Did anything change?"** — if the plan changed since you last
  opened the app, a banner tells you so you're never caught off guard

Before the trip starts, this screen shows a countdown. On the first
morning it announces **IT'S TRIP DAY!!** — because it is.

And at the bottom lives the **🍰 Dessert First** button — a family
doctrine turned feature. Tap it and the app offers one delightful,
low-disruption wildcard from your wishlist: *"There's room for one
little adventure. Take it?"* Take it (it joins today's plan, with
confetti), roll again, or save it for another day. It never wrecks
the schedule. It just makes the day 10% more legendary.

### 🔎 The Big & Simple view (a.k.a. the Mom Button)

At the bottom of Today there's a button that opens today's plan in
**huge text** — just the stops, the times, and the addresses. No menus,
no buttons, nothing to accidentally tap. Perfect for parents,
grandparents, kids, or anyone who just wants to know *"where are we
going and what time do I need to be ready?"*

### 🗓️ Plan — build the itinerary

- Tap **+** to add a stop. Start typing in the **map search** and pick a
  result — it pins the stop to the map automatically with the address
  filled in.
- Every stop can hold: category (restaurant, coffee, hotel, mountains,
  concert, show, hidden gem, gas, 17 in all), arrival & departure
  times, cost, opening hours, website, parking, accessibility notes,
  and your own notes.
- **Priorities:** ⭐ Must do · 👍 Would like to · ⏳ If we have time.
  Must-dos get a badge everywhere. If-we-have-times wait quietly for
  free moments.
- **Hotels are special** — pick the 🏨 category and you get fields for
  confirmation number, check-in/check-out, phone, and Wi-Fi. That's what
  feeds the "Tonight" card on the Today screen.
- **The wishlist** at the bottom holds stops with no day yet. Found a
  cool thing but don't know when? Wishlist it, sort it later.
- Reorder any day with the ▲▼ arrows.
- **✨ Ask the assistant** — type things like *"find attractions between
  Kingman and Flagstaff"* or *"great cheap lunch near downtown Nashville"*
  and get real suggestions you can add to the wishlist with one tap.
- **💵 Budget** — set a total budget (captains), and anyone can log
  expenses in seconds: amount, category (hotels, food, gas, tickets,
  parking, shopping), optional note. You get spent / remaining /
  per-traveler totals and a "where it went" breakdown. Anyone can add;
  you can delete your own; captains can tidy up.
- **⭐ Worth it?** — open any stop after you've been and answer three
  quick taps: Worth the money? Worth the time? Would you go again?
  It's your family's travel history, not anonymous internet reviews —
  and the Trip Brain learns from it.
- **Export** downloads your whole trip as a file. Your data is always yours.

### 🗺️ Map — see the whole trip

Every pinned stop on an interactive map, with a colored driving route,
total drive time, and mileage. Filter by day or view the whole trip.
Tap any pin for details and a directions link.

### 📸 Feed — the Family Feed

A private timeline for the trip. *"Mom found the world's largest
pistachio."* *"Charles accidentally ordered six tacos."* *"This pie is
worth driving 50 miles for."* Post a moment (with or without a photo),
heart each other's posts, and build the trip's story as it happens.
It's not social media — nobody outside the trip ever sees it, and it
exports with the rest of your trip data. When the memory-book feature
arrives, these moments become its raw material.

### 👥 People — this is the special sauce

- **"How are you doing?"** — tap your status: ✅ I'm ready ·
  ⏳ Need 10 min · 🍔 Hungry · 🚻 Bathroom stop · 🪫 Low energy ·
  🙅 Skipping this one · 👋 Go without me…
- **Group pulse** shows everyone's status at a glance: *"5 of 6 ready."*
  No group chat with 47 messages. No standing in a hotel hallway
  shouting "ARE WE LEAVING?"
- **My Travel Rhythm** — a short quiz about how you travel: pace,
  mornings, walking distance, food restrictions, spending comfort,
  must-dos, hard-nos. **Every question is skippable, and skipped
  questions simply vanish from your profile** — no blanks, no guilt.
  Each answer you *do* give has a privacy setting:
  - **Shared with group** — everyone on the trip sees it
  - **Captain only** — just the trip captain sees it (great for "my
    knees can't do six-mile hikes" without a group announcement)
  - **Just for me** — nobody sees it but you
- **Invite travelers** (captains only) — pick what new people can do,
  get a code and a link, and text it to them:
  - **Can edit** ✏️ — full co-planner
  - **Can suggest** 💜 — they can propose stops; you approve or decline
  - **View only** 👀 — they see everything, touch nothing

### 🗳️ Decide — votes with a timer

Someone has to pick lunch. Start a vote, add options, set a timer
(2 minutes for parking-lot decisions, up to 24 hours for big ones).

- Everyone sees a live countdown.
- **No response counts as "I'm good with whatever wins"** — so one
  person lost in a gift shop can't hold six people hostage.
- Ties go to the captain. The captain (or whoever started the vote) can
  also close it early.

## Roles: captains and travelers

- **The captain** (you, usually) can edit everything, invite people,
  approve suggestions, manage members, and settle ties. A trip can have
  more than one captain — promote a co-planner in People → Manage.
- **Travelers** see everything and participate in statuses and votes;
  what else they can do depends on the permission you gave their invite.

## What you're paying for (the honest, plain-English version)

Your app has two bills. Here's what they actually are:

### 1. Render (~$7/month) — the "always-on phone"

Your app has to *live* somewhere. Think of it like this: imagine the app
runs on a phone that has to stay on, plugged in, connected to the
internet, 24 hours a day, forever — so that when your mom opens the app
at 6 a.m., it answers. You wouldn't want that phone to be *your* phone.

**Render is a company that keeps that "phone" running for you** in a
big professional building (a data center). That's all a "server" is:
a computer that's always on, whose whole job is answering when your
app is opened. The $7/month rents you that computer, plus the little
hard drive attached to it where your trips, accounts, and photos are
saved. That hard drive is why your stuff is still there tomorrow.

### 2. The Anthropic key (pay-per-use, usually pennies) — the AI brain

The ✨ assistant — "find attractions between Kingman and Flagstaff" —
isn't smart by itself. When you ask it something, your app quietly asks
a powerful AI (named Claude, hi 👋) and relays the answer back.

The **API key** is like a prepaid calling card for talking to that AI.
Every question costs a tiny amount (fractions of a cent). Ask fifty
questions a month and you might spend a dime. **Nothing else in the app
uses it** — the maps, routing, weather, and everything else are free
services. No key? The app still works fine; only the ✨ assistant
politely sits out.

**What's free:** the maps, driving routes, mileage, weather, and map
search all come from free public services (OpenStreetMap and friends).
No Google bills, ever.

## Little questions, quick answers

**Do my travelers pay anything?** No. They make a free account and join
with your code. All costs live on the owner's side.

**Is my stuff private?** Yes. Trips are visible only to the people on
them. Your rhythm answers respect the visibility you chose. Data sits
on your Render disk.

**What if two people edit at once?** The app refreshes itself every 30
seconds while you're on a trip, so changes flow to everyone quickly.

**Can I use it with no signal?** Partly — pages you've already loaded
keep working, but new searches, maps, and syncs need a connection.
A true offline mode is on the roadmap.

**Something looks broken?** Pull down to refresh first. Still weird?
Tell Audrey — the fix usually ships the same day, and your app updates
itself automatically when it does.

## The Trip Brain 🧠

On your trips home screen there's a **Trip Brain** card. The app
quietly notices your travel style from your *real* trips — not generic
travel tips: your favorite kinds of stops, how packed your days run,
whether coffee is non-negotiable, how decisively you mark must-dos.
Tap "What has it noticed?" and it tells you. It gets sharper with
every trip, stop, vote, and Feed post — and if the AI key is on, its
observations get extra witty.
